I am an incoming M.S. student in Computer Science at the [University of California, San Diego](https://ucsd.edu/). I earned my B.Eng. in Communication Engineering through the joint program between the [University of Electronic Science and Technology of China](https://www.uestc.edu.cn/) and the [University of Glasgow](https://www.gla.ac.uk/).

My work focuses on trustworthy and practical AI systems, including LLM evaluation, retrieval-augmented generation, multimodal AutoML, diffusion models, and AI for communication systems. I enjoy building research ideas into reliable, measurable software.

Recent highlights include a paper accepted at **IEEE BIBM 2025**, a **top-3% (16/4,315)** finish in the Kaggle Playground Series irrigation-need competition, and engineering projects spanning LLM/RAG evaluation, multi-agent code review, and production-style backend systems.
